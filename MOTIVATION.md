# Motivation

**30 Days of RAG** is a hands-on series where each day ships a small, runnable project that demonstrates one retrieval pattern, agentic workflow, or debugging technique.

## Why this series exists

Most RAG learning resources stop at “hello world” demos. In real projects you quickly run into:

- retrieval quality issues (chunking, hybrid search, reranking)
- routing and tool use
- evaluation and debugging
- operational constraints (latency, cost, local vs managed)

This repo is my attempt to bridge that gap with small projects you can actually run, tweak, and extend.

## Principles

- **Runnable first:** each day should be executable end-to-end.
- **Small surface area:** keep the core idea visible (minimal boilerplate).
- **Pragmatic defaults:** sensible settings and clear extension points.
- **Production mindset:** when relevant, include evaluation, tracing, or failure diagnostics.

## Who it’s for

- engineers moving from “chatbot demos” to RAG systems
- students building portfolio projects
- teams prototyping retrieval strategies before committing to a stack
